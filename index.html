English Grammar Rules

The foundation of B1 is mastering the basics.

The Present Simple

Use Subject + Verb. For regular verbs, add -s only for he/she/it. (e.g., She plays soccer). For irregular verbs, memorize the forms.

The Present Continuous

Use am/is/are + verb-ing. It is used for actions happening now or temporary situations. (e.g., I am reading a book).

The Past Simple

Use Subject + Verb-ed. It is used for completed actions in the past. (e.g., He went to the store yesterday).

The Future Simple

Use Subject + will + verb. It is used for predictions, promises, or future plans. (e.g., I will call you).

Subject-Verb Agreement

Ensure the verb matches the subject number. Singular (He) + singular verb, Plural (They) + plural verb.

B1: Intermediate Strategies

Top Tips for Level B1

Level B1 is where you move from "understanding the main points" to "speaking with confidence". Here is how to improve:

  • 🗣️
    Focus on Pronunciation. Don't just memorize words; learn the sounds (Phonics). Use apps like Duolingo or Anki to track your progress.
  • 📚
    Read "B2" level articles. Start with simple news sites (BBC Learning English) and gradually move to more complex topics like tech or politics.
  • 💬
    Speak daily. Talk to yourself in the mirror or record a 1-minute video about your day. Speaking is the best way to learn grammar rules.
